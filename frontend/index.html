SIMPLE PROCESS

How ReLife Works

Five simple steps to give products a second life.

👤

1. Create Your Profile

Register on ReLife as an individual or organization.

📦

2. List or Post a Need

List a product for sale, exchange or donation, or post what you need.

🔍

3. Discover and Request

Browse available products and send a request for what you need.

🤝

4. Connect with the Owner

The product owner reviews your request and accepts or rejects it.

💚

5. Complete the Handover

Arrange the handover and mark the reuse as completed.
